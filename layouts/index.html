Research Interest

  1. 01

    Verified AI programming

    AI agents that produce not only implementations but proofs of their correctness, by pairing AI programming with formal verification.

  2. 02

    Specification languages for verified AI programming

    Writing specifications is the bottleneck in verified AI programming. I design specification languages that stay precise and easy to read.

  3. 03

    Neuro-symbolic agent safeguards

    Verifying the safety of an AI agent's actions with formal techniques, and building guardrails on top of those verifications.
